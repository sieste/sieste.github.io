Summary

I am a statistician and data scientist working on evaluating and improving forecasts of weather and climate. My earlier work developed methods for forecast verification, recalibration and Bayesian inference. Since 2025 my research has centred on AI weather prediction, combining rigorous verification with modern deep learning, largely in partnership with the Met Office.

As Director of Business Engagement and Innovation I lead the department's work with industry and government, including Knowledge Transfer Partnerships, Met Office research and executive education, and the 195th European Study Group with Industry, which I brought to Exeter in 2026. I am a Turing Fellow of the Alan Turing Institute.

Research

My research develops statistical and machine-learning methods to evaluate and improve forecasts from complex computer models of weather and climate. A central aim is to quantify forecast skill and uncertainty rigorously, so that forecasts can be trusted by the people who use them.

AI weather prediction and high-impact weather

Running AI weather models at reduced numerical precision; adding AI model ensembles to the Met Office's operational IMPROVER blend; neural-network prediction of Saharan dust and of North Atlantic dry intrusions; impact-based forecasting of windstorms and tropical cyclones.

Forecast verification and post-processing

Statistical tests, scoring rules and Bayesian frameworks for verifying and recalibrating ensemble forecasts, and the R package SpecsVerification. This work is the basis of my chapters in both editions of the reference text Sub-seasonal to Seasonal Prediction (Elsevier, 2019 and 2025).

Bayesian inference for environmental data

With colleagues at the University of Iceland I co-developed Max-and-Smooth, a fast two-step method for approximate Bayesian inference in latent Gaussian models, applied to flood frequency analysis and spatial forecast post-processing. Related work includes Gaussian vector fields on meshes for downscaling, and dynamic linear models for SARS-CoV-2 surveillance in wastewater.

Current directions

AI foundation models for weather and climate; uncertainty quantification for AI forecasts; formally verified software for AI safety.

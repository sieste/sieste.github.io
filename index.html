Stefan Siegert

I'm an Associate Professor in Data Science at the University of Exeter. I have a PhD in Physics from TU Dresden, followed by two post-docs and a lectureship in Statistics at Exeter.

I try to be good at being a husband, father, scholar, athlete, artist — to varying degrees of success. See my now page for what I'm up to at the moment.

I was born in Germany in 1984. I now live in Exeter/Devon (UK) with my wife and three kids.

Academic work

My students and I work on the theory and application of statistical modelling and machine learning. We develop mathematical methods that help us use observation data and computer simulations to understand the past and predict the future. Most of our recent work is on AI weather prediction, much of it in partnership with the Met Office.

I am Director of Business Engagement and Innovation in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, and a Turing Fellow of the Alan Turing Institute. I lead the department's work with industry and government, including Knowledge Transfer Partnerships, Met Office research and training, and the 195th European Study Group with Industry (Exeter, 2026).

I teach statistics, data science and machine learning, from first-year modules to MSc level and executive education for the Met Office.
